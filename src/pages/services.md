  ### Garden Design Consultations


  Working with you to establish your overall requirements and creation of initial outline proposals


  *I﻿t starts with a conversation, and a visit to your garden.  We'll talk through any ideas or aspirations you have, and identify any specific requirements or constraints that we need to bear in mind.  Armed with that crucial information, we'll put together a few initial sketches and ideas, and discuss together what you like, and what you'd like to change.*






  ### Full design of your perfect outdoor living space


  Full design service to make your garden your favourite room in the house, to include space for eating, relaxing and entertaining


  *O﻿nce we've established and agreed the outline, we can then proceed to a more detailed design, including specifications for any landscaping work that may be needed.  We'll also include details for planting, with the aim of sustaining interest and colour all year round - and selecting plants that suit the amount of care and maintenance you want to commit to.*






  ### Project Management to bring your new garden design to life


  Once the design is agreed, we will work with suitable providers to ensure the design is fully realised and you can start living the dream!


  *T﻿his is where the magic happens.  You'll see the transformation of your space, and be able to enjoy the benefits of your investment for many years to come.*
